The founder shouldn’t have to guess what their packaging says.

LabelProof is an early-stage startup concept based in the UAE. We are exploring whether an independent, evidence-linked second check — AI-assisted and human-reviewed — helps founders print bilingual packaging with more confidence.

Who it’s for
Non-Arabic-speaking founders of small imported packaged-food brands in Dubai and Sharjah.
When it helps
Before launching a new product, adding a new SKU or redesigning packaging — just before the print run.

What is LabelProof?

LabelProof is a proposed AI-assisted, human-reviewed service that compares your English packaging artwork, Arabic packaging artwork and product specifications to identify potential inconsistencies before you print. It is currently at concept stage and being tested through a pilot programme.

Who is it designed for?

Primarily non-Arabic-speaking founders of small imported packaged-food brands in Dubai and Sharjah. We also want to hear from importers, distributors, packaging agencies, Arabic food-label linguists and food regulatory professionals.

What documents can I submit?

For the pilot we expect English packaging artwork, Arabic packaging artwork (print-ready PDF or high-resolution image) and your product specification sheet — ingredients, allergens, net weight and claims. Please don’t upload anything through this website; we will agree a secure way to share files with each pilot participant.

Does LabelProof replace a translator?

No. LabelProof is a verification layer that checks whether the two language versions say the same thing. It does not translate or write your Arabic copy, and it works alongside your translator and designer.

Who performs human review?

Qualified Arabic food-label linguists review Arabic wording, meaning and consistency, and food regulatory specialists review relevant regulatory concerns within their expertise. We are currently recruiting these review partners. Only findings actually reviewed by a person are shown as Human-reviewed.

Does LabelProof guarantee UAE regulatory compliance?

No. LabelProof provides preliminary verification support only. It does not provide official regulatory certification, approval or a guarantee of compliance. Matters requiring confirmation should be taken to the relevant authority or a qualified adviser.

Who pays for LabelProof?

LabelProof is planned as a B2B service. Food brands, importers, distributors and packaging agencies would pay. The planned models are pay-per-label, bulk SKU packages and subscriptions. Pricing will be set with pilot participants, so there is no published pricing yet.
